Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Donating Items

What items are currently needed?

Nonperishable Food, menstrual products, personal hygiene essentials, and diapers and baby care items. The checklist above can help you plan what to bring.

When can I drop off donations?

Donation drop-offs run from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM on the listed October, November, and December dates. You can also donate on November 25 and December 23 from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Can I donate on a community pantry day?

Yes. Donations are welcome on November 25 and December 23, 2026, during pantry hours: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM Pacific.

Can I bring opened products, perishable food, or other items?

The posted list includes nonperishable food, menstrual products, personal hygiene essentials, diapers, baby wipes, and unopened, unexpired infant formula / baby milk. Please email us first about product condition, packaging, expiration dates, or items outside that list.

Does the checklist send a donation pledge?

No. It’s a personal planning tool saved in this browser when browser storage is available. It does not send your selections to us or reserve any items.

Planning Your Visit

Where do I bring my donations?

Bring donations to Tina’s Kitchen at 582 S Murphy Ave, Sunnyvale, CA 94086, during the scheduled drive hours.

Where can I find parking or accessibility information?

Tina’s Kitchen is in a shopping plaza with a large parking lot. Get Directions. For entrance access or other accessibility questions, please contact us before your visit.

Where should I check for schedule notes?

Check Schedule Notes & Advisories before your visit. Drive hours apply only to the dates listed on this page.

Pantry Support & Pickups

Who is the community pantry for?

The community pantry is open to community members in need. Pantry days are November 25 and December 23, 2026, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Do I need to register or bring anything to visit the pantry?

For questions about registration, what to bring, or your specific situation, contact our team before your visit.

Can you pick up my donation if I can’t drop it off?

Yes, you can request a donation pickup by email. Include your general area, donation items and quantities, and preferred dates. Pickup requests are subject to availability. We’ll confirm arrangements by email.

Other Ways to Give

Can I give money instead of items?

Yes. Use the Givebutter donation button or QR code to donate online. Please include “The Giving Table” in the note with your donation. You’ll complete your donation on Givebutter.

Is my donation tax deductible? Can I get a receipt?

Yes. Breaking Silence Movement is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and donations are tax deductible to the extent permitted by law. We can provide a donation receipt—please email us to request one. Keep your receipt for your records.

Can I volunteer for this drive?

Yes! Email hello@breakingsilencemvmt.org to ask about volunteering. Include your availability and how you’d like to help so we can discuss opportunities.

Can my workplace or community group organize a collection?

Email hello@breakingsilencemvmt.org to discuss a group collection and coordinate a drop-off.
